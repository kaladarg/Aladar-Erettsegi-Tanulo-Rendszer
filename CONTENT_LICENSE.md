## What the MIT license covers

The [MIT license](LICENSE) covers the **source code** of Érettségi Gyakorló only: everything in this repository, including the app, the tools in `tools/`, the JSON schemas, the documentation and the self-written demo content pack.

## What it does not cover

The official érettségi exam papers, their attachments (forrásanyagok, mellékletek) and the marking schemes (javítási-értékelési útmutatók) are published by the **Oktatási Hivatal** (Hungarian Educational Authority) at [oktatas.hu](https://www.oktatas.hu/kozneveles/erettsegi/feladatsorok). They belong to the Oktatási Hivatal. Many papers also contain third-party works (literary texts, images, maps, sources) that are protected by their own authors' rights.

- These materials are **not** stored in this repository and are **not** licensed under MIT.
- Structured data derived from them (task text, answer keys, explanations, coordinates) lives in a separate, currently **private** content repository.
- That repository will only be made public once its reuse has been cleared with the Oktatási Hivatal.

## Using your own copy

Students can download papers from oktatas.hu themselves and open them in the app. Imported files stay in the student's browser and are never uploaded anywhere.

## Corrections and takedown requests

If you are a rights holder and believe material is used incorrectly, please open an issue or contact the maintainer through the repository; we will respond promptly.
